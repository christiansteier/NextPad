NextPad help

NextPad is the successor of Etherpad at Hochschule Düsseldorf (University of Applied Sciences Düsseldorf). Several people write in the same text at the same time and see each other’s changes immediately.

Joining a pad

Enter the name of the pad and choose “Open or create”. The same name means the same pad. If the name does not exist yet, NextPad creates a new, empty pad. Allowed are letters, digits, spaces and the characters - _ . ( ) , up to 50 characters.

Starting from a template

Templates create a pad with a random name and an outline for group work or minutes. To invite others, open “Share and export” at the bottom left of the pad and choose “Copy link”, on a phone also “Share via device”; whoever opens the link can write along.

Started from Moodle

Pads, quizzes and idea collections started as an activity from Moodle show “Back to course” at the top. The link leads to the course page in Moodle, also from the embedded view.

Name and profile picture

Without signing in, a person appears as “Guest” in the pad. A different name can be entered under “Participants”. Anyone who signs in through Moodle appears with their name and profile picture from Moodle.

Earlier versions

The timeline shows how the text came about and replays the changes. The owner of a pad (whoever created it on the start page; in Moodle, the teachers) can bring back an earlier version with “Restore this version”. It is added as a new change on top; the version before stays in the timeline.

Export

“Export” offers the text as HTML, Markdown, plain text, PDF or a print view.

Markdown

Pasted Markdown text is formatted, for example headings with #, lists with -, tasks with - [ ], **bold** and tables with vertical bars (| A | B |, below it | --- | --- |).

Indenting

Tab indents the paragraph or heading by one level, Shift+Tab outdents it; the buttons “Indent” and “Outdent” do the same. Selected paragraphs move together. In lists, Tab nests the item further; in tables, Tab moves to the next cell.

Tables

The table icon inserts a table with three rows, three columns and a header row. With the cursor in a table, the “Table” menu adds or deletes rows and columns and switches the header row on and off. The Tab key moves to the next cell.

Rating

The thumbs icon inserts a rating with thumbs up and thumbs down at the cursor, for example after a suggestion. Each person has one vote per rating; a second click takes it back, a click on the other thumb changes it. The counts also appear in the export. Unlike in the idea collection, the browser counts these votes; for binding votes, use the idea collection or Moodle.

Table of contents

The H icon on the right of the toolbar shows a table of contents of all headings on the left; a click jumps to the heading. “Insert into pad” also puts the table of contents into the text. It updates itself and appears in HTML, Markdown and PDF, in the PDF with page numbers.

Who can see a pad?

Everyone who knows its name or link. Pads from the start page are not protected by a password. For courses with fixed groups, the pad activity in Moodle is the better choice; there, only course members see the pads.

Language

NextPad is available in German and English. “DE | EN” at the top of the page and in the pad’s toolbar switches the language; this browser remembers the choice for a year. Without a choice, the language from Moodle applies, otherwise the browser’s. The text in pads is not translated. Exports (PDF, print view, text, Markdown, ideas CSV) follow the chosen language. The email before deletion comes in the language last chosen while signed in through Moodle, otherwise in German and English.
